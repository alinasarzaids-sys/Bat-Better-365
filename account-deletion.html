Account & Data Deletion Request

At CricTool, we value your privacy. If you would like to delete your account or any personal training data associated with your profile, you can do so directly in the app or by contacting our support team.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Instant)

✅ Recommended — Delete directly in CricTool

  1. Open the CricTool app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm
  5. Your account and all data are permanently removed immediately

Option 2: Email Request

Step 1: Send an email to our support team:

Email us at:

admin@crictoolofficial.com

Subject: "Account Deletion Request"

Step 2: Include the following in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Specify whether you want to delete your entire account or just specific training/activity data

Step 3: We will process your request

Once received, we will permanently remove your data from our servers within 7 business days.

⚠️ Important: This Action Cannot Be Undone

Once your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed from our systems. You will not be able to recover your training history, progress, or any other data. If you wish to use CricTool again in the future, you will need to create a new account and start from the beginning.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, we will permanently remove the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, password, profile details
  • Training Data: All training session records, drills completed, performance metrics, and progress tracking
  • Journal Entries: All journal entries and personal notes
  • Calendar Events: All scheduled training sessions and calendar events
  • AI Coach Conversations: All conversation history with the AI coach
  • Achievements & Progress: All earned achievements, skill levels, and streaks

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions, please contact us:

  • Email: admin@crictoolofficial.com
  • Subject Line: "Account Deletion Inquiry"
  • Response Time: Within 7 business days
